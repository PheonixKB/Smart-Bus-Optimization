# Project Context

## Business Mission
To optimize public transportation systems by leveraging data science and operations research to reduce wait times, increase efficiency, and improve passenger satisfaction.

## Target Users
- Transit agency planners and operations managers
- City transportation departments
- Public transit riders (indirectly through improved service)

## Strategic Objectives
1. Reduce average passenger wait time by 20% within the first year of deployment.
2. Increase bus fleet utilization efficiency by 15%.
3. Provide actionable insights for route planning and schedule adjustments.
4. Enable data-driven decision making for transit agencies.
5. Scale the solution to multiple cities and transit systems.

## Key Success Metrics
- Wait time reduction (minutes)
- Fleet utilization rate (%)
- On-time performance improvement (%)
- Passenger satisfaction score (survey-based)
- Cost savings from optimized operations

## Assumptions
- Historical data is available and representative of normal operations.
- Transit agencies have the technical capability to deploy and maintain the system.
- External factors (traffic, weather) are either incorporated or treated as noise in the model.

## Constraints
- Must comply with data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR) when handling passenger data.
- Solution must be integrable with existing transit management systems.
- Real-time processing is not required for MVP; batch processing is sufficient.
- The system should be operable with minimal training for transit agency staff.

## Out of Scope
- Real-time vehicle tracking (Phase 2)
- Mobile applications for passengers (Phase 2)
- Multi-modal integration (e.g., combining with trains, ride-sharing)
- Fare optimization and pricing strategies
